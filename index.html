Questions, answered

Is WellBridge free?

Yes. Check-ins, the library, the assistant and counselor booking are free for students.

Who can see my check-ins?

Only you, unless you turn on care team contact. Then verified, approved responders can see flagged check-ins and reply. Counselors see a mood summary only if you share it.

Is the AI assistant a therapist?

No. It gives quick tips and points you to the right service. It cannot diagnose or handle emergencies.

What if I’m in crisis?

Tap Urgent help any time to call emergency services, campus security or a crisis helpline.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can ask for a copy or deletion of your data at any time.
